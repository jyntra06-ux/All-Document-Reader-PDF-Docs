4. Permissions We Request

The application requests the following device permissions to provide its core document, scanning and notification features. You can review or revoke most of these at any time in your Android device settings.

Storage Access

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (used only on Android 9 and earlier, up to API level 29) and MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE ("All files access") allow the application to browse, open, edit, save and organize documents stored anywhere on your device, including outside the app-specific folder. This broader access is requested because the application functions as a general document/PDF manager rather than one limited to a single folder. It is used to act on files you choose to open, save or manage, and is not used to read your files in the background without your action.

Photos and Media

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES is used on Android 13 and above to let you select images from your device to scan, convert to PDF, or insert into a document.

Camera

The application does not directly request the device CAMERA permission. Instead, when you use the scan feature, it launches your device's existing camera app (via an image-capture intent) to take a photo, which is then returned to the application for processing. Camera hardware is declared as an optional feature, so the app remains usable on devices without a camera.

Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS is used to deliver notifications to your device via Firebase Cloud Messaging, such as feature updates or reminders. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Advertising ID

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID allows the application to access the Android Advertising ID, used for ad personalization, measurement and fraud prevention as described in the Advertising section below.
